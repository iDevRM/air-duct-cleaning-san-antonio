What Happens When You Call?

No phone tree and no hard sell. Here is the whole sequence:

  • We ask roughly how many vents you have and whether the home runs one HVAC system or two.
  • We ask what prompted the call — dust, a smell, a remodel, a dryer taking two cycles.
  • We give you a realistic range on the spot and tell you what could move it.
  • We book a walkthrough. The tech shows you inside a duct run before quoting a firm number.
  • If the walkthrough says you do not need the service yet, we tell you that and there is no charge.
